About

based in Sehwan, Jamshoro

I've spent most of my career on the teaching side of IT — first as a computer instructor, later as visiting and full-time faculty, and currently as a Primary School Teacher (PST) under Sindh's education department. Alongside that, I've kept building: a diploma and short courses in IT, an internship in Android development, and a six-week stint in PTCL's technical department gave me a hands-on foundation that classroom teaching alone doesn't.

Over the last couple of years that hands-on side has turned into a small personal practice: shipping Android apps that solve problems I run into directly — a ledger for tuition fee recovery, a prayer-times companion, and more on the way. I care about tools that are small, offline-friendly, and actually get used.

    Fee Recovery Ledger

    An offline-first ledger for tuition centres to track student fees and dues without spreadsheets. Data is stored locally with Room and synced to the cloud through Firebase, with an optional export to Google Sheets for centre owners who still like a paper trail.
